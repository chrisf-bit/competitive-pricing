Structural Changes Extract - August 2026
Three pieces of client structural feedback have been implemented on branch release-2-partner-detail. This document lists every change, from before to after, for review. The build is clean: tsc and vite.
Summary
1. Fictional locations: Every real city or country has been replaced with a fictional place, so no location maps to the published parity list.
2. Real room counts: The 10 lead hotels now show the real room numbers from Partner Data Set 46.
3. Expedia to Key OTA: Every learner-facing reference to Expedia has been replaced with Key OTA.
1. Fictional locations
One fictional location is used per property across all of its parity-regime variants, so location no longer signals a regime. There is no gameplay impact: location is display-only, while parity is driven by a separate internal field.

Deliberately not changed
* Internal, non-rendered strings: Code comments, scenario step IDs, and the internal competitor token still say expedia or hold old city names in comments. None of these appear to the learner.
* KAM company names: Names such as Thames Hospitality Group and Aravalli Hotels carry mild regional hints. These were left as-is and can be neutralised if needed.
* Contact names: Names such as Liam O'Connell and Anton Müller were unchanged because they were out of scope for this pass.
* Cultural flavour in one parked record: Yuki's ryokans / Komorebi references were left unchanged because they are not city or country names and the record is not shown.
